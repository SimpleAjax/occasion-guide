# Occasion Guide: competitive landscape

**Research date:** 14 September 2026  
**Purpose:** identify the products a visitor is likely to use instead of an India-first, respectful occasion guide. This is a product comparison, not an assessment of the correctness of any religious or cultural guidance.

## Short answer

There is no obvious single product that combines occasion explanation, a practical family preparation path, visible regional variation, and a pre-moderated place to ask a question. The market is split between calendars/ritual reference, paid ritual fulfilment, parenting content, and open-ended Q&A. That split is the opportunity, but it also means the product must earn trust through editorial review and careful moderation.

## What the market leaves open

1. **The answer is scattered.** A family may use a Panchang for the date, a parenting blog for a baby milestone, a local contact for the ritual, and WhatsApp/Quora/Reddit for variations. No reviewed, occasion-neutral preparation path is apparent across these public offers.
2. **Variation is usually buried.** Calendar tools tailor dates to location; parenting articles sometimes mention regional practice. The product can make variation a first-class part of every guide without declaring one household’s practice universal.
3. **Coordination and discussion are weakly connected.** Ritual marketplaces can arrange a service and wedding tools can organise a large event, while editorial articles have no structured question route. The proposed shareable household checklist plus pre-moderated “question / regional note / correction / accessibility tip” flow is a meaningful trust boundary.

## Three sharp differentiation recommendations

1. **Own “prepare respectfully,” not “the one correct ritual.”** Put the three-step Preparation Path—Before, On the day, After—at the centre of each guide. Beside it, state what commonly varies and give three questions the reader can ask their own elder, priest, organiser, or place of worship.
2. **Make provenance visible, not decorative.** Every factual section needs a source, editor/reviewer, review date, and a correction route; community notes must be clearly labelled as a person’s regional or family experience. This is the clearest answer to the trust gap between generic content and open Q&A.
3. **Build a moderated knowledge-and-coordination layer before a marketplace.** Start with signed-in submissions and human approval, then promote repeatedly verified regional notes into editorial updates. Add a shareable household checklist with roles and decisions. Do not sell samagri, vendors, astrology or bookings in v1: that preserves neutrality and keeps the experience useful to families with different budgets and traditions.

## Scope guardrails suggested by the comparison

- Use Drik Panchang or equivalent authoritative calendar sources for date verification; do not calculate or promise auspicious timing in v1.
- Do not repeat prescriptive ritual instructions from commercial or generic articles without independent editorial verification and a clear variation note.
- Treat child-care advice as safety-sensitive: link to appropriate medical/child-safety authorities where needed, and do not present cultural content as health advice.
- Avoid an open comment model at launch. Reddit’s own public materials illustrate why rules, reporting, active moderation, tooling and human review are operational—not optional—requirements.
